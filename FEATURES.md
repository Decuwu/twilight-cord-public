# Twilight-Cord: current features (1.26.0)
A lightweight client mod for Discord Stable and PTB on Windows.
Download: https://github.com/Decuwu/twilight-cord-public/releases/latest

## Messages
- **MessageLogger**: deleted messages stay in red, edits in yellow, with a searchable log.
- **Notes**: save any message to your own note pages.
- **Remind me** and **copy as markdown**: right-click a message.
- **Translate**: one click (Google, or DeepL with your own key), plus /translate.
- **AutoCollapse**: folds long messages.
- **LinkCheck**: folds links that show one website but open another.
- **BetterCodeblocks**: language and line count on code blocks.
- **Top ^**: jump to the first message of a chat.

## People and servers
- **PlatformIndicators**: desktop, phone and web icons next to names.
- **StaffTags** and **RoleDividers**.
- **MemberCount**: members and online count above the member list.
- **FriendNotifier**: friend removals and changes, and watched friends coming online.
- **EmojiStealer**: save emojis and stickers or add them to your server.
- **Quick mute** and **mark everything as read**.

## Commands and typing
- **/download**: the video from a TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, YouTube or X link, attached as a file.
- **/tag**: save text and send it by name.
- **/petpet**: pet-pet GIF of someone's avatar.
- **SilentTyping** and a **words-per-minute** counter.

## Look and privacy
- **ThemeMaker**: fonts, text size, avatar shape, compact chat.
- Menu styles, and **Make it your own** to rename it and swap the logo.
- **NoTrack** and **ClearURLs**: block analytics, strip tracking from links.
- **ImageZoom** and **ViewIcons**.

## The mod itself
- Menu (Ctrl+T on new installs) with plugin search and sorting.
- Discord opens where you last had its window.
- Auto-updates with a changelog, and a check after Discord updates.
- Built-in feedback form.
- **Quest completer** (off by default): against Discord's rules, your account can be banned.
